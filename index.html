Transpose works like retuning a guitar: −1 sounds like E♭ tuning, −2 like D tuning, +2 like a capo on fret 2. Key names stay the same, and the real pitch shows under the note.

Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P play notes, Z and X move the octave.
